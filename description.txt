Terraria AMS – Advanced Ammo Management

Stop digging through your inventory for ammo mid-fight.

Terraria AMS (Advanced Ammo Management) introduces a radial ammo selection wheel that lets you quickly swap between ammunition types without opening your inventory.

Inspired by weapon wheels seen in modern games, AMS makes ammo switching fast, intuitive, and combat-friendly while staying true to the feel of Terraria.

Features
Radial Ammo Wheel

Quickly select ammo using a centered radial UI that appears instantly while playing.

Dedicated Ammo Slots

Store multiple ammo types in a separate management system without cluttering your inventory.

Seamless Weapon Integration

Weapons automatically use the currently selected ammo type from the wheel.

Fast Switching During Combat

Swap between ammunition types in real time without opening the inventory menu.

Lightweight & Performance Friendly

Designed to integrate smoothly with tModLoader without affecting gameplay performance.

How It Works

Open the ammo wheel.

Select the ammo type you want to use.

Fire your weapon normally.

Your weapon will automatically consume ammo from the selected slot.

No more inventory searching.
No more accidental ammo switching.
